---
title: How VM on Golem Works
description: A high level overview of providers, requestors, discovery, payments, VMs, and SSH access.
---

VM on Golem connects people who need virtual machines with people who can host them. Requestors rent compute. Providers supply compute.

## Main actors

Requestors use the web app. They connect a wallet, browse providers, create payment streams, rent VMs, and connect over SSH.

Providers run provider software. They publish capacity, verify payment streams, create VMs, expose SSH access, monitor operations, and withdraw earned funds.

Discovery connects the two sides by letting requestors find currently available providers.

## Basic system flow

1. A provider starts its service and measures available host resources.
2. The provider publishes an advertisement through discovery.
3. A requestor subscribes to discovery and sees matching providers.
4. The requestor selects resources and opens a payment stream to the provider.
5. The provider verifies the stream.
6. The provider provisions a VM and configures SSH access.
7. The requestor connects over the assigned SSH host and port.
8. The stream pays the provider while the rental remains funded.

## Why streams matter

Streams make payment continuous instead of a one time checkout. The provider can verify that payment exists before creating compute, and can react if payment expires.

## Why networking matters

VM creation and SSH access require direct provider reachability. Discovery can show that a provider exists, but the requestor still needs to reach the provider endpoint and SSH port to use the VM.
