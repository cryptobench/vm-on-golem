---
title: Streaming Payments
description: Understand deposits, stream rates, runway, top ups, termination, and provider withdrawals.
---

Streaming payments fund VM rentals over time. Instead of paying once for an unknown duration, the requestor deposits funds into a stream that vests to the provider according to a rate per second.

## Deposit

The deposit is the amount of token placed into the stream. It defines how much total value is available to pay the provider.

A deposit that is too small creates little runway and can cause the VM to lose funding quickly.

## Rate per second

The rate per second determines how quickly funds vest to the provider. Larger VMs and higher provider prices require higher rates.

The requestor app calculates or proposes a stream rate based on provider advertised pricing and selected resources.

## Runway

Runway is the estimated time before the stream can no longer pay at its current rate. Requestors monitor runway to decide when to top up.

Providers monitor runway to avoid hosting unpaid compute.

## Top ups

A top up adds more funds to an existing stream. It extends runway without replacing the VM or stream relationship.

Top ups require wallet approval and gas.

## Termination

Termination stops future payment. Requestors should terminate streams when they are finished with a rental. Providers should remove compute when payment is no longer valid.

## Withdrawals

Providers withdraw funds that have vested from streams. Withdrawal requires a transaction, so providers need gas on the payment network.
